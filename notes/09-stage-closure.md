# Stage Closure — Exact Fibonacci / String-Net Benchmark

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Status:** Closed research stage

This note is deliberately short. Detailed calculations are in `notes/01` through `notes/08` and the corresponding `experiments/` scripts.

## Decision

**NEGATIVE BENCHMARK.** The un-enriched exact Fibonacci/category workstream is closed.

Across F/pentagon reconstruction, R/hexagon/braid transport, Levin–Wen plaquette factorization, adjacent-plaquette commutation, and graphical proof-certificate extraction, the explicit-history representation has not demonstrated mathematical or computational structure unavailable to an optimized categorical graphical/tensor formulation.

## The decisive boundary

The recurring obstruction is the coherence quotient:

\[
\boxed{
\text{coherence-invariant history data}
\to
\text{existing categorical semantics},
}
\]

while

\[
\boxed{
\text{history-sensitive data}
\to
\text{requires additional semantics}.
}
\]

Therefore merely increasing system size is not an admissible next step.

## Reopening criterion

Reopen this line only if the new work introduces at least one explicit enrichment with an auditable semantics, for example:

- computation/proof cost;
- provenance or dependency sets;
- memory/communication locality;
- approximation or coherence defect;
- physical time/control/noise constraints.

The natural mathematical form is a resource-enriched history groupoid, where coherence classes supply equivalent implementations and the enrichment supplies a nontrivial optimization or diagnostic objective.

## Research value of the negative result

The benchmark did not merely fail to find an advantage. It identified why the original hypothesis repeatedly collapses to known structure, and it supplies exact regression tests for any future enriched theory.

In particular, future claims can now be tested against three hard baselines:

1. direct optimized categorical F/R equations;
2. local string-net tensor/factor representations;
3. the classical constant-size graphical proof of adjacent plaquette commutation.

Any renewed AEG claim must beat or enrich these baselines rather than a weaker surrogate.
