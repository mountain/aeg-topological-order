# Problem Statement

## Primary experiment

Can AEG turn fusion-category coherence into an explicit, sparse, computable operation-history geometry without losing the categorical invariants?

## Minimal benchmark

Use the Fibonacci fusion grammar

\[
\tau\otimes\tau=1\oplus\tau
\]

and reconstruct F-data from coherence rather than importing it.

## Main questions

1. What is the minimal AEG data structure corresponding to a fusion history?
2. How should fusion-channel vector spaces be attached to history vertices?
3. Can F-moves be treated as local transports with ordinary categorical gauge appearing as frame gauge?
4. Does the pentagon become an exact higher-cell flatness condition?
5. Can the representation reduce the computational burden of solving coherence equations?
6. After adding braiding, can global braid holonomy be computed naturally from local history transitions?
7. Can Levin–Wen plaquette operators be compiled from the history grammar?
8. If no computational gain exists, does the explicit-history representation still reveal a useful residual structure hidden by categorical quotienting?
